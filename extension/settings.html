Settings

Sites foxcache may read

foxcache reads a site only after you allow it here, and only when you click Capture. It reads with the session your tab already has, and only sends GET requests: it never sends, edits or deletes anything there.

What foxcache keeps

Conversations, projects (name, instructions and file names), memories, and custom instructions. Never a password, a cookie or a token, and never a file's contents. Everything stays in this browser.

foxscope may ask for your memories, preferences and project names, and you approve each request there. It can never get your conversations.

Delete everything

Delete every captured record and every capture in progress from this browser. The sites themselves keep your data.
